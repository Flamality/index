# Community Guidelines

Welcome to the platform! Our mission is to provide a **safe, inclusive, and engaging environment** for all users. These Community Guidelines apply to all users, content, and interactions across the platform, including the website, apps, subdomains, and related services (“the platform,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

### By using the platform, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, or permanent bans.

## 1. Treat Everyone with Respect

The platform is designed for collaboration, learning, and creativity. Respectful communication is essential.

**Do:**

- Communicate politely and professionally, even when disagreeing.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences, and backgrounds.
- Provide constructive feedback rather than personal attacks.

**Do not:**

- Harass, bully, intimidate, or threaten other users.
- Use discriminatory, offensive, or inflammatory language.
- Target individuals or groups based on identity, beliefs, or personal characteristics.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Politely disagreeing with someone’s idea in a discussion thread.
- ❌ Sending repeated private messages to harass a user.
- ❌ Posting derogatory comments about someone’s identity.

---

## 2. Share Appropriate Content

All content posted on the platform must be suitable for a general audience.

**Do:**

- Share original content or properly credit others.
- Post content that contributes positively to discussions or community engagement.

**Do not:**

- Share pornography, graphic violence, or illegal material.
- Post pirated content, malware, or links to harmful software.
- Share sensitive personal information of yourself or others without consent.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Posting an educational video or article with proper credit.
- ❌ Sharing copyrighted movies, software, or cracked programs.
- ❌ Uploading content containing graphic violence.

---

## 3. Maintain Honest and Safe Interactions

Trust is critical for all platform interactions.

**Do:**

- Provide accurate information when registering or using platform features.
- Report unsafe or suspicious content to the moderation team.

**Do not:**

- Impersonate other users, staff, or external organizations.
- Engage in scams, phishing, or fraudulent activity.
- Spread false information intentionally.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Reporting suspicious links or scam messages to moderators.
- ❌ Pretending to be platform staff to gain personal information.
- ❌ Posting false announcements or misinformation.

---

## 4. Protect Privacy

User privacy is a core principle.

**Do:**

- Share only information you are comfortable disclosing.
- Respect the privacy and anonymity of other users.

**Do not:**

- Post someone else’s personal information without explicit consent.
- Attempt to access private accounts or data without authorization.
- Record or share conversations without permission.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Asking permission before sharing a user’s content externally.
- ❌ Posting a user’s phone number or home address.
- ❌ Accessing another account without consent.

---

## 5. Platform Integrity

The platform exists to provide safe and legitimate services.

**Do:**

- Use all features as intended.
- Report bugs, vulnerabilities, or exploits responsibly.

**Do not:**

- Exploit, hack, or manipulate platform features for unfair advantage.
- Spam, flood, or disrupt normal platform operations.
- Circumvent security measures or restrictions.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Reporting a bug that allows unintended access to private content.
- ❌ Using bots to spam comments or artificially inflate engagement.

---

## 6. Legal Compliance

Users must comply with all applicable laws.

**Do:**

- Respect copyright, trademark, and other intellectual property rights.
- Follow local, national, and international regulations while using the platform.

**Do not:**

- Post or distribute illegal content.
- Encourage or participate in illegal activity or violations of intellectual property rights.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Sharing links to content with proper licensing or permission.
- ❌ Uploading pirated movies, software, or music.

---

## 7. Reporting and Moderation

The platform moderation team ensures compliance and safety.

**Do:**

- Report content or behavior that violates these guidelines.
- Cooperate respectfully with moderation actions.

**Do not:**

- Publicly argue with moderators about enforcement.
- Attempt to bypass warnings, suspensions, or bans.

**Moderation actions may include:**

- Warnings and notifications
- Temporary account restrictions or feature limitations
- Permanent account suspension for severe or repeated violations

**Reporting Process:**

1. Use built-in reporting tools to flag content.
2. Provide clear, factual information about the violation.
3. Moderators will review and take appropriate action.

---

## 8. Create a Safe and Positive Environment

We aim for an engaging, welcoming platform for all users.

**Do:**

- Encourage collaboration, positivity, and inclusivity.
- Support new users in understanding platform features.

**Do not:**

- Promote self-harm, dangerous behavior, or unsafe challenges.
- Spread hate, threats, or intimidation.
- Engage in toxic or hostile interactions.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Offering helpful guidance to a new user.
- ❌ Posting threatening or abusive messages.
- ❌ Sharing dangerous challenges or instructions.

---

## 9. Community Responsibility

All users contribute to the platform’s health.

**Do:**

- Encourage respectful discussion and positive engagement.
- Report content that could harm other users or the platform.

**Do not:**

- Ignore violations that could negatively impact others.
- Attempt to manipulate, exploit, or deceive other users.

**Examples:**

- ✅ Reporting spam or harassment in a timely manner.
- ❌ Coordinating campaigns to harass or manipulate users.

---

## 10. Updates to Guidelines

These guidelines may be updated periodically to reflect new laws, platform features, or community needs. Users are responsible for reviewing and complying with the latest version.

By using the platform, you acknowledge and agree to these rules. For additional details, please review our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
